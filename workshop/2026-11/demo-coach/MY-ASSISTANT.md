# Juniper — your assistant

Set up 17 November 2026. Keep this file; it's your reference if something stops working.

## Who they are

Juniper is your personal assistant. Start a session in this folder, say hello, and they'll
answer as someone who knows you. You talk to them in plain language — no commands, no special
syntax. They won't speak until you do — a quick "good morning" is all it takes.

They don't do everything themselves. They have specialists they hand work off to:

- **Scheduling** — finds times that actually work, writes the message proposing them, and tells
  you when a day is too full. It never puts anything on your calendar itself.
- **Writing** — drafts your client emails in your voice, including the awkward ones. You always
  read and send them yourself; nothing goes out without you.
- **Research** — public background on a company or a person before you meet them, with sources
  and a clear line between what's confirmed and what's a guess. It only looks at public
  information and won't search on anything your client told you in confidence.
- **Meeting prep** — pulls together what you need before a client session: where you left off,
  what's still open, and what to watch for, from the client's Bloom once you've connected it.
  Short enough to read in the parking lot.

You don't need to pick a specialist. Just say what you need and Juniper sorts it out.

## Connecting Bloom

Juniper can read your clients' Bloom: their last Weekly, To-Dos, Quarterly Priorities, KPIs,
O&O list and org chart. With it, meeting prep and your morning brief start from what each team
actually committed to, not just from your notes. To connect it, once:

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and choose **Add custom connector**. (On a Team or
   Enterprise plan without that button, your workspace owner adds Bloom once first.)
2. Call it Bloom, and paste `https://mcp.bloomgrowth.com/mcp` as the address.
3. Sign in to Bloom. On Bloom's confirm page, pick **your own coaching firm**, not a client.
4. Start a new conversation and ask Juniper: "Which clients can you read in Bloom?"

One connection is enough. It reaches every client you coach that has turned on AI access, so if
you connected Bloom more than once before, remove them all and connect once more, from your own
firm. A client missing from the list hasn't turned on AI access yet.

Juniper only reads Bloom. They never add or change anything there: no To-Dos, no O&O items, not
in a client and not in your own firm. If something needs adding, Juniper drafts it and you add
it.

## If something goes wrong

**I said hello and Juniper wasn't there — it answered like a stranger.**
Make sure you've opened the same folder as before — Juniper lives in this one, and a session
started in a different folder won't have them in it. If the folder is right, quit the app
completely, reopen it, and start a new session. (And remember Juniper speaks second: they
answer your first message rather than greeting you before you've typed anything.)

**Juniper says they can't see my calendar (or email).**
The connection may have timed out. Open the "+" menu and reconnect it, then start a new
conversation.

**Juniper says they can't read a client in Bloom.**
Ask Juniper which clients they can read in Bloom. If none, Juniper will say which of two things
it is. Bloom was connected from somewhere other than your own firm: remove it and connect again,
picking your firm (see *Connecting Bloom*). Or none of your clients has turned on AI access yet:
nothing to fix on your side. If the list has others but not that client, that client hasn't
turned on AI access yet.

**Juniper feels like a different person / forgot who I am.**
Tell them: "read your notes about me." That usually sorts it.

**Claude says I'm running low on usage, or asks me about switching models.**
Claude comes in a few sizes, and the app shows the current one near the message box — a name
like "Opus" or "Sonnet." Bigger sizes use up your plan's allowance faster, and Juniper doesn't
need the biggest one for day-to-day work. If you're bumping into limits, click that name and
pick **Sonnet** — Juniper stays exactly who they are, remembers everything, and your allowance
goes a lot further. You can switch back any time.

**Something else.**
Email Mike Benner at michael.b@bloomgrowth.com. Tell him what you asked Juniper for and what
happened instead — that's usually enough to sort it out without a call.

## Changing things

Just ask Juniper directly — "I'd like you to be more formal", "call me by my first name",
"add a specialist for research". You don't need to edit anything yourself.
