# Pre-work email

**Send:** Thursday 6 August, morning. Later than that and the weekend is the only runway left.
**To:** the 15 coaches, individually addressed if the tooling allows — a group send reads as an
announcement and gets skimmed.
**Deadline given to coaches:** Sunday, deliberately. It's soft, and it leaves Monday to chase the
stragglers while Mike is travelling.

Three notes on why this is shaped the way it is:

- **No GitHub link, and no mention of GitHub.** A coach who lands on the repo page sees a wall of
  files and a green **Code** button whose most obvious option, *Download ZIP*, produces a folder
  that looks right and does not work. Tuesday's install is a name typed into a box. Keep it that way.
- **One reply covering all three asks**, not three separate confirmations. Every extra round trip
  costs compliance.
- **The plan check is first** because it's the only one that can't be fixed in the room. No paid
  plan means no Code tab, and that coach spends two hours watching a neighbour.

---

**Subject:** Before Tuesday — 15 minutes of setup (please reply when done)

Hi [First name],

Tuesday morning you're going to build yourself an assistant. Not a chatbot you ask questions —
something that knows your practice, remembers your clients, and hands work off to specialists you
choose. By noon it'll be doing real work for you.

To spend the session building instead of installing, I need about **15 minutes** from you before
then. Three things.

**1. Make sure you're on a paid Claude plan.**
Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise — any of them is fine. The free plan won't work for this; the part
of Claude we're using isn't available on it. If you're not sure which you're on, open
[claude.ai](https://claude.ai), click your name in the bottom-left corner, and look at Settings →
Account.

This is the one thing I can't fix for you on the day, so it's the one I'd most like you to check
now.

**2. Install the Claude desktop app.**
Download it at **[claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download)** and sign in with the same
account as above. The browser version won't do — we need the desktop app specifically.

**3. Windows only — install Git for Windows.**
If you're on a Windows machine, also install this: **[git-scm.com/downloads/win](https://git-scm.com/downloads/win)**

Click through the installer accepting every default. You will never use this program directly and
you don't need to understand it — Claude needs it running underneath to work on Windows. Takes
about three minutes.

On a Mac? Skip this one entirely.

---

**Then reply to this email with two things:**

1. **"Done"** — or tell me which step you got stuck on
2. **Mac or Windows?**

That second one takes you three seconds and saves me guessing which half of the room needs which
instructions.

If anything above throws an error or just doesn't look like what you expected, **reply and tell me
rather than pushing through it.** A two-minute exchange now beats twenty minutes of us both
troubleshooting live while thirteen people wait.

Aiming to have everyone squared away by Sunday.

See you Tuesday,
Mike
