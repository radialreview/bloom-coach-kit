# Run of show — Tuesday 10:00–12:00, open floor 1:00–2:30

**Minimum viable outcome** (from the build brief, unchanged): every coach leaves with an
assistant that is *named*, has a *personality*, completed *one successful delegation*, and has
*one scheduled task created*. Everything else is bonus.

**The two hard rules:**

- **Never demo a routine by waiting for its scheduled time** — dispatch has a randomized
  multi-minute delay. Always use **Run now**.
- **The projector shows Mike's screen doing exactly what the coaches do**, one step at a time.
  "Your screen should look like this" is the most effective sentence in the room.

---

## 9:40 — Doors / pre-flight (Stephanie)

- Sweep the room: app installed? Signed in? Windows machines get the Git check (triage #1).
- Coaches with no Code tab (free plan — Mike has the unconfirmed list from pre-work replies):
  pair them with a neighbor **now**, not at 10:15 when it's disruptive.

## 10:00 — The promise (Mike, 10 min)

By noon, three things will be true: your assistant has a name and knows your practice; it did a
real piece of work for you; and starting tomorrow it writes you a morning brief on a page you
bookmark. Show your own page from this morning — 15 seconds, no tour. Then the commitment-card
teaser: "before lunch, each of you reads one sentence aloud."

## 10:10 — Install together (15 min)

Projector, one step at a time: Settings → Plugins → **Add** → **Add marketplace** → type
`radialreview/bloom-coach-kit` → install → open a **new session** in a plain folder
(Documents is fine — *not* a code project folder). Stephanie sweeps with the triage sheet.
Nobody moves on until neighbors confirm the skill shows up when they type `/`.

## 10:25 — The interview (25 min)

Everyone runs `/bloom-coach-kit:meet-your-assistant` at once. Mike narrates the six questions
from his own screen but **does not rush the room** — the practice question (Q4) is where the
assistant gets good, say so out loud. Coaches who finish early: tell them to answer their
assistant's first question honestly instead of helping neighbors — Stephanie does the helping.

## 10:50 — The hello (10 min)

Everyone: new session in the same folder, type "good morning." Let the room have the moment —
this is the emotional peak of the day; don't talk over it. Then one instruction: *"Tell it one
true thing about a client — a name, where they are in the Growth Plan. That's how it learns."*

Close the segment with the owner's manual, 30 seconds, verbatim:

> One more thing before we put them to work. During setup, your assistant wrote you an owner's
> manual — a file called **MY-ASSISTANT.md**, sitting in your folder. Ask for it right now, in
> plain language: *"show me my owner's manual."* It'll pull it up.
>
> That file is for three weeks from now, when something looks off and I'm not in the room:
> what each of your specialists does, things to try, what to do if the greeting stops, and who
> to email. You never edit it — if you want something changed, tell your assistant.

Why "ask for it" rather than "find the file": it works identically on Mac and Windows, it takes
zero file-system knowledge, and it reinforces the day's one lesson — everything goes through
the assistant. (It's a real file too — Documents folder, opens like any document — for the
coach who wants to print it.)

## 11:00 — Connect + first delegation (20 min)

- **+** menu → connect **calendar** (and email if the room is moving well). Browser OAuth,
  approve, new conversation.
- Then one plain-language request each, their choice: *"What does my week look like?"* or
  *"Draft a follow-up to [real client] about their Quarterly Priorities."*
- Call out what happened structurally, once, in one sentence: "It handed that to a specialist.
  You'll never need to know which one."

## ✂ 11:25 — THE CUT LINE

Behind schedule? Drop in this order — never compress the hello or the interview:

1. **Add-a-specialist segment → open floor** — it was born there and lives there happily;
   slides 10–11 just get skipped.
2. **Email connector** — calendar alone carries the demo; email lands in open floor.
3. **Morning-brief setup moves to open floor** — the MVO's "scheduled task" can be created for
   the room by showing Mike's already-running one and building theirs at 1:00.
4. **Delegation goes demo-only** — Mike's screen instead of everyone's, coaches run theirs at
   open floor.

On schedule? Proceed:

## 11:25 — Set up my morning (20 min)

Everyone runs `/bloom-coach-kit:set-up-my-morning`. Two questions, then the skill insists on a
supervised **Run now** — coaches approve every prompt that appears (say it plainly: "this is
you giving your assistant standing permission — you do it once"). The run ends with their page:
**everyone bookmarks it before anything else happens.** Phones out is fine — the bookmark is
the retention mechanism.

## 11:45 — Grow the team: add a specialist (10 min — the flex segment)

Promoted from open floor after the dry run came in fast; it's cut #1 if the day says otherwise.
Everyone hires the same specialist, because every coach has this exact problem: session notes
that go nowhere. Slides 10–11 carry the script — the coach says, in their own words, *"After my
sessions I scribble notes and never do anything with them — I want a specialist who turns my
brain-dump into a recap."* Then narrate the three beats as they happen: it asks about the
output (answer honestly), it proposes a name (take it or rename), it plays the job back (say
yes). New session, and tell them to feed it real notes from a real session this week — the
recap lands in their assistant's memory, which makes every future prep brief better. Close
with the point: *"That's how you hire from now on. Find a job, describe it, done."*

## 11:55 — The commitment (5 min, no cards)

No paper — better anyway: they tell the one who'll actually remember. Slide 12 has the
sentence: *"[Name] will [recurring task] every [cadence]."* Each coach writes it in one
message to their assistant — **"hold me to this"** — then the read-around: name, sentence,
pass, applause once at the end. **Stephanie writes every commitment into the log** — that's
the two-week follow-up list. The assistant has it in its notes; the morning brief will bring
it up. Then lunch.

## 1:00–2:30 — Open floor

No agenda on the wall; the actual agenda:

- Fast coaches: hire *another* specialist — they know how now; this time from their own real
  practice (proposals, invoice nudges, testimonial collection).

  - **Remote Control** (per the build brief — nothing to set up).
  - **Mike's Command Deck** — 60 seconds on the live page: check a box, click one link back to

    don't need yet. When you've got enough systems feeding you, this is where it goes —
    that's webinar two."* Do not tour it; the point is that it exists and it's real.
- Mike floats; Stephanie keeps the question log going — it closes out as the webinar backlog.
