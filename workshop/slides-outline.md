# Slides outline — build Sunday, after the frozen-code run

Fourteen slides. The rule that decides slide vs screen:

- **SCREEN** when Mike is *doing* something — coaches watch a real thing happen once.
- **SLIDE** when coaches are *doing* something — the instruction stays projected the whole
  time they work, because half the room looks up mid-step and needs to re-find their place.
- Never narrate over a working room. Put it on the slide and walk the floor.

Slide design rules (for Sunday): one instruction per slide, type readable from the back row,
no decoration that competes with an instruction. The demo is the production value.

**NOTE CARD** blocks are what Mike says, written to be glanced at, not read aloud verbatim.

> **NOTE CARD:** Two hours from now you'll have hired someone. Not installed something —

### Slide 2 — By noon, three things are true
**On slide:** three lines: *Your assistant knows your practice · It did real work for you

> **NOTE CARD:** Point at line three — this one's my favorite. It doesn't wait for you to
> show up. Every weekday morning it writes up your day on a page you'll bookmark. Mine ran

### → SCREEN (15 seconds, no tour)
Mike's own morning-brief page from this morning. Real calendar, real names visible only if
comfortable — otherwise scroll past fast; the *existence* is the point.

> **NOTE CARD:** This is mine from this morning. Yours starts tomorrow. That's the whole
> pitch — let's build it. (FLIP BACK TO SLIDES — do not linger, do not tour the page.)

### Slide 6 — DO: Ask for the manual
**On slide:** Say: **"show me my owner's manual"** · That file is for three weeks from now ·
Never edit it — ask your assistant to.

> **NOTE CARD:** It wrote you an owner's manual during setup. Three weeks from now when

## Build notes for Sunday

- Slides 3, 8, 9 are the workhorses — they stay up 10–15 minutes each. Proof them on the
  actual projector from the back of the actual room.
- The `radialreview/bloom-coach-kit` string on slide 3 is the only typed text of the day —
  make it monospace, huge, and dead-accurate. A typo there is fifteen support tickets at once.
- Slide 7's diagram: redraw with the room's most common assistant names penciled in? No —
  keep it generic; coaches map themselves.
- Total: 14 slides, ~6 minutes of Mike's screen. If slide-building runs long Sunday, cut
  slides 12–14 to plain text on the room's whiteboard — they're logistics, not payload.
