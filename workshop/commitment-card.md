# Commitment card — print 20 on card stock, one per coach plus spares

Purpose: the card is the bridge between "neat workshop" and "changed habit." One sentence,
written by hand, read aloud to the room, photographed. The photos are the follow-up list — in
two weeks, Mike checks in on exactly what each card says, nothing else.

Print: quarter-page cards, heavy stock, landscape. Big blanks — coaches write with pens, not
pencils.

---

## Card front

<br>

> ## My assistant's first standing job
>
> <br>
>
> **____________________** will **________________________________________________**
> <sub>(assistant's name)</sub> <sub>(the recurring task — one thing, specific)</sub>
>
> <br>
>
> every **____________________**.
> <sub>(cadence — "weekday morning", "Friday afternoon", "before each Bloom Weekly")</sub>
>
> <br>
>
> Signed: ____________________

## Card back

> **Already true when you wrote this:** your morning brief runs every weekday — that page you
> bookmarked. This card is the *second* job.
>
> When you get back to your desk, tell your assistant about this card in plain language.
> That's it — that's how everything works from now on.

---

## Facilitator notes

- Hand cards out **at 11:50, not earlier** — a card handed out at 10:00 gets written at 10:05,
  before the coach knows what their assistant can do.
- The best cards name a real client or a real meeting. If someone writes "help me be more
  productive," ask them: *"What did it just do for you in the last hour?"* — then have them
  write that.
- Read-around: name + sentence, no commentary between readers, applause once at the end.
- **Photograph every card before lunch.** Assign it to Stephanie — Mike will be mid-conversation
  with someone, guaranteed.
- Two weeks out, the follow-up to each coach quotes their own card back to them. That message
  gets replies that "how's it going with the AI?" never will.
