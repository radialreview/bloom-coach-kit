## If something goes wrong

**I said hello and {{NAME}} wasn't there — it answered like a stranger.**
Make sure you've opened the same folder as before — {{NAME}} lives in this one, and a session
started in a different folder won't have them in it. If the folder is right, quit the app
completely, reopen it, and start a new session. (And remember {{NAME}} speaks second: they
answer your first message rather than greeting you before you've typed anything.)

**{{NAME}} says they can't see my calendar (or email).**
The connection may have timed out. Open the "+" menu and reconnect it, then start a new
conversation.

**{{NAME}} feels like a different person / forgot who I am.**
Tell them: "read your notes about me." That usually sorts it.
