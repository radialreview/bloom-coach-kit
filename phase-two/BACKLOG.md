# Backlog — future webinar / demo material

Ideas banked with honest notes. Nothing here ships without its caveats being solved.

## Outreach / growth (pairs with the five-calls-a-day discipline)

- **Growth-calls ingredient in the morning brief** — SHIPPED 0.3.3. Window from calendar
  gaps, target count, names only from notes. The webinar follow-up: teach coaches to feed
  the pipeline ("tell your assistant every prospect conversation") so the nudge graduates
  from time-block to named list.
- **LinkedIn outreach specialist** — reads the coach's own connections via the Chrome
  extension (their real logged-in browser) and suggests 2–3 people worth a call, with a
  drafted opener each. **Constraints that shape it:** attended sessions only — browser
  automation is the fragile class the Command Deck deliberately excludes from unattended
  runs, and LinkedIn actively fights automation, so this stays a coach-present,
  read-and-suggest specialist, never a scheduled scraper and never an auto-messenger
  (draft-never-send applies doubly here). Demo-first candidate for the outreach webinar.
- **Call logging loop** — coach says "log my calls: reached Dana, left a message for Sam";
  assistant writes them to notes; the brief's nudge shows streak and gaps. Zero new
  machinery — this is persona memory behavior, teachable in a webinar without a release.

## Connector show-and-tells (the "true personal assistant" beat)

- **DoorDash MCP** — the crowd-pleaser demo: assistant orders lunch during the webinar.
  Real usefulness is thin for the coaching practice, but as a "connectors mean anything in
  the menu" demonstration it lands harder than another calendar example. Purchasing flows
  need the coach's confirmation on-screen — that's a feature of the demo, not a bug: it
  shows the assistant drafts and the human approves, same rule as email.
- **Strava connector** — the personal-health angle: brief ingredient "you've got a gap at
  noon and you haven't moved since Tuesday." Good example of the roster growing past the
  practice into the coach's whole working life — which is the retention story in month
  three.

## Time-blocking, generally

The scheduler already flags crowding; the inverse — proactively **reserving** blocks
(calls, prep, deep work) — is a natural scheduler upgrade. Constraint: propose-never-book
stays absolute; the specialist drafts the calendar holds, the coach confirms. Candidate for
the same outreach webinar as the growth-calls material.
